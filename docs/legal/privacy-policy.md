## The short version

Peek-a-Piece does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information from
anyone — children or adults. Everything the app stores (the photos a parent
adds, and which puzzles have been finished) stays on the device. There are no
accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

## What the app stores, and where

| Data | Purpose | Where it lives | When it's deleted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Photos a parent selects | Turned into jigsaw puzzles for the child | Copied into the app's private storage on the device | When the parent deletes the photo in the app, or uninstalls the app |
| Which puzzles have been completed | Shows a check mark on finished puzzles | On the device (local key–value storage) | When the puzzle is reset in the app, or the app is uninstalled |
| Sound volume, timer, and puzzle-size settings | App preferences | In memory only, for the current session | When the app is closed |

None of this data leaves the device. It is never uploaded, backed up to a
server, or transmitted to us or to anyone else.

## What the app does NOT do

- No user accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking.
- No advertising and no ad networks.
- No third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No location collection.
- No microphone or camera use. (Photos are chosen through the operating
  system's own photo picker; the app only receives the images the parent
  explicitly selects.)
- No sale or sharing of data — there is no data to sell or share.

## Children's privacy

Peek-a-Piece is designed for young children and is intended to be set up by a
parent or guardian. Because the app collects **no** personal information from
any user, it is compliant with:

- the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** in the United States;
- the **UK Age-Appropriate Design Code** (Children's Code);
- the **EU General Data Protection Regulation**, including provisions for
  children (**GDPR-K**).

Photo selection and all other configuration are placed behind a simple
parental gate (a short arithmetic question) so a child cannot add or remove
photos or change settings on their own.

## Permissions

- **Photo library access** is requested only when a parent taps "Add Photos",
  and is handled by the operating system's photo picker. The app receives only
  the specific images selected.
- The Android build declares the standard `INTERNET` permission (a technical
  requirement of the app framework). The app makes no network requests and
  transmits no data.

## Data retention and deletion

All app data is local to the device. To remove it:

- **A single photo:** open the parent area and delete that photo — its file is
  removed from the device.
- **Everything:** uninstall the app. This deletes all stored photos and
  progress.

We hold no copies of anything, so there is nothing for us to delete on our end
and no data-access request to make.

## Third-party content

The app bundles a small set of illustrated starter puzzles and one background
music track (licensed under the Pixabay Content License — "Music by Dmitrii
Kolesnikov from Pixabay"). These are shipped inside the app and involve no data
collection.
